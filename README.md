# ProductStrategistAgent · Scout

> **选品策略师 (Product Strategist)** — The research-and-strategy entry point of a digital-product pipeline. Turns trending topics into validated, sellable product opportunities.

**Persona: Scout** — the scout who spots opportunities worth selling.

[简体中文](README_cn.md)

---

## What it does

Produces an *Opportunity Brief* covering: trending topics → fitting digital products → market research → competitor analysis → monetization model → traffic & sales channels.

## Pipeline position

① **Scout** research → ② Wright build → ③ Buzz marketing → ④ Vendy sales → ⑤ Echo review

## Built-in skills

- `hot-trend` — global trend scanning & product selection
- `anysearch` · `find-skill` — general search & skill discovery

## Constraints

Focus on passive income (no courses / services / agency operations); channels follow Payloadz + PayPal / Xiaohongshu (Gumroad / TikTok unavailable).
